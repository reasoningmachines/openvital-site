02 / In the works

A first step, with more to explore.
In development

Meet Medproof.

A clearer picture of your medical bill.

We’re building a privacy-first tool to compare an itemized medical bill with an insurer’s explanation of benefits, and surface potential discrepancies for you to review.

  • Potential duplicate charges and billing mismatches
  • Evidence and context behind each flag
  • Draft dispute letters and call scripts you send yourself

We’re building Medproof for iPhone. Planned capabilities may change as development progresses.
